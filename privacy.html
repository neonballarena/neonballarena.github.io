Privacy Policy

Neon Ball Arena · Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Neon Ball Arena mobile game ("the app") uses and why. The app has no user accounts and the developer does not run any servers that receive your data.

Photos and the camera

You can put a picture on a ball by choosing a photo or taking one with the camera.

  • Photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker. The app only receives the pictures you select and does not request access to your photo library.
  • The camera permission is requested only when you choose to take a photo. You can deny it and still use the rest of the app.
  • Selected pictures are resized and saved only inside the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded or shared by the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices and local law.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Options → Privacy Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Google settings.
  • Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Buying "Remove Ads" or "Pro" turns off full-screen ads between matches. Optional "watch an ad" rewards are only shown when you tap them.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play which products you own and keeps a copy of that on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores its settings (such as sound volume and game options), your prediction statistics, which products you own and the pictures you added. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
